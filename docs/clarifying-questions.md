# Clarifying questions sent to GC Career Studio

Sent before starting the build, per Step 1 of the assignment. The MVP was
built in parallel on the assumptions documented in the main README, rather
than waiting for a response.

**Business & audience**
1. Who's the primary audience — students, early-career professionals,
   senior/executive career changers, or a mix? Any specific industries you
   specialize in?
2. Do you operate in a specific country/region (for compliance, currency,
   testimonials, etc.) or serve clients globally?

**Services**
3. What are the core services (resume review, LinkedIn optimization, mock
   interviews, career coaching, job-search strategy, etc.), and do you want
   each as its own detail page or grouped on one page for the MVP?
4. Do you sell one-off sessions, packages/bundles, or subscriptions?

**User journey & objectives**
5. What's the single most important action you want a visitor to take — book
   a free discovery call, fill a lead form, or something else?
6. Is the primary goal of this site lead generation, or do you also want it
   to build authority/credibility (blog, resources, case studies)?

**Pages/features**
7. Do you have existing testimonials, case studies, or success metrics
   (e.g., "80% of clients land interviews within 30 days") to feature, or
   should placeholders be used?
8. Should pricing be shown publicly, or discussed on a call?

**Branding**
9. Do you have an existing logo, color palette, or brand guide?

**Lead generation & booking**
10. Do you already use a scheduling tool (Calendly, Cal.com, etc.), or should
    one be recommended?
11. Where should lead-form submissions go — a specific inbox, CRM, or
    spreadsheet?

**Technical & scalability**
12. Do you have a domain name and hosting preference already, or is that
    open?
13. Given the future SaaS direction, should the structure prioritize support
    for user accounts/dashboards later (consultants, applicants, clients),
    even though the MVP won't include auth?
